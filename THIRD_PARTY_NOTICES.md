## Simple Icons

When Devicon does not provide a colour original, Cutting Board uses a mark
from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), version
16.28.0.

Simple Icons is made available under the
[CC0 1.0 Universal dedication](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md).
Its project disclaimer notes that individual brand marks may be subject to
their owners' copyright, trademark, or brand-use rules even though the Simple
Icons collection is released under CC0. The marks in Cutting Board are used
only to identify the corresponding technologies; no endorsement or affiliation
is implied. All product names, logos, and brands remain property of their
respective owners. See the upstream
[Simple Icons disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md)
for details.

Lucide interface artwork and Simple Icons fallbacks are rasterised into
transparent local PNG files at development time. Devicon-supported technology
marks render from its locally bundled WOFF font. The application neither
downloads icons nor renders SVG at runtime. The generic-service artwork was
drawn specifically for Cutting Board and is distributed under this project's
license.

The names and logos represented by these icon sets may be trademarks of their
respective owners. They are used only to identify detected technologies; no
endorsement or affiliation is implied.
